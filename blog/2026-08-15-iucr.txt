~ A single reference point for the three protein structure tools and their source, collected here for IUCr 2026.
~ ProtNRD is an interactive visualisation of protein backbone geometry, built on LAI invariants. It plots how backbone properties co-occur across thousands of high-quality PDB structures, conditioned on local sequence context. It runs in two modes: Pairwise, the geometry of one amino acid conditioned on a neighbouring residue at a chain separation of zero to four; and Trimer, the geometry of one position within a three-residue sequence context.
~ Every layout is encoded in the URL fragment, so the address bar is always a shareable link to exactly what is on screen. The app link above opens a preconfigured six-panel layout rather than an empty workspace.
~ Two companion tools sit alongside it. Torsion is an interactive 3D visualiser for dihedral angles along a polypeptide backbone. RamaCube is a three-dimensional Ramachandran explorer for backbone dihedral space.
~ The invariants themselves are calculated by the BRI extension. The release link carries the ProtNRD source and setup instructions; the databases are hosted separately on Hugging Face and linked from there.
